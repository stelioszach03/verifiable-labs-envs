# V-Certified ↔ ISO/IEC 42001 crosswalk

**Crosswalk version**: `v0.0.1` (Phase 31.E)
**Framework version**: ISO/IEC 42001:2023

ISO/IEC 42001 is the first international standard for AI management
systems. It specifies requirements for establishing, implementing,
maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system within
the context of an organisation.

The V-Certified programme provides a thinner, evidence-driven
attestation that does not replace ISO 42001 certification but maps the
V-Certified control set onto specific ISO clauses so customers
maintaining ISO 42001 conformance can reuse evidence collected for that
audit.

## How to use this crosswalk

For each V-Certified control, the table below lists the ISO 42001
clauses it satisfies. Customers undergoing a V-Certified audit submit
the listed evidence kinds (per `attestation_artifacts.kind`); the
auditor checks each kind against the relevant clause.

The full machine-readable mapping is served at:

    GET /v1/standards/iso_42001

## Control mapping

| V-Certified control | Title | ISO 42001 clauses | Evidence kinds |
|---------------------|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| VC-1.1 | AI training-data provenance | A.5.2, A.6.1, A.7.1 | training_doc, audit_report |
| VC-2.1 | Model evaluation rigor | A.7.4, A.8.2 | monitor_record, rm_record, prm_record |
| VC-3.1 | Change management trail | A.9.1, A.9.2 | change_mgmt |
| VC-4.1 | Legal accountability | A.10.1 | legal_signoff |

## Notes

- VC-2.1 evidence kinds (monitor / RM / PRM records) are produced by
  Phase 28 monitors + Phase 29-30 reward / process-reward services.
  Customers using these services accumulate the required evidence
  automatically.
- ISO 42001 clauses A.5-A.10 cover the AI management system policy +
  organisational + supporting + operational + performance-evaluation +
  improvement controls. The V-Certified mapping focuses on the
  data-quality + evaluation-rigor + change-management subset most
  relevant to model training data attestation.
- Future crosswalk versions will broaden coverage to A.4 (context of
  the organisation) and A.10.2-A.10.4 (incident response + compliance
  monitoring).

## Conformance level

V-Certified attestation alone does NOT confer ISO 42001 certification.
Customers seeking ISO 42001 certification must engage an accredited
certification body. V-Certified evidence may be reused as input to that
certification audit.
